Pokljuka Gorge

Pokljuška soteska

~2 hCircular
FreeNo ticket
DryGorge floor

Vintgar has the river and the crowds. This one has neither — the water that carved it disappeared underground long ago, so you walk along the bottom of a dry limestone slot with the walls closing to arm's width above your head.

It is the better walk of the two, and hardly anyone knows it. Ten minutes up the road from the house, in the village of Krnica.

The route

Start in Krnica and follow the signs up through the forest along the bed of the Ribščica stream.

Into the gorge on wooden walkways — the narrowest stretch is called the Gallery, and it is exactly as good as it sounds. Further up you pass a natural rock bridge, duck through a cave, and cross a hanging footbridge.

Two versions. A short loop turns back at the top of the gorge; the full circuit carries on up into the forest and comes back round. Both are signed from the village.

Good to know

  • ◆Boots. The walkways are wooden and the rock stays damp — both get slippery after rain.
  • ◆Cool even in August. The gorge holds cold air all summer, so take a layer even when the valley is baking.
  • ◆Good with older children, who love the cave and the bridge. Less good with toddlers.
  • ◆No facilities at all — no kiosk, no toilets, no phone signal in the gorge itself.
