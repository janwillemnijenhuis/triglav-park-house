Seven places to eat

Two in the village, four around the lake, and one worth the drive. Nearest first.

Gostilna Vintgar

  • 2 km
  • Slovenian
  • €€
  • Terrace

Your local, and one of the oldest inns in the whole Bled area — it has been feeding people here since the early 1800s. Trout done properly, mushroom soup, and a terrace big enough that you rarely have to wait. Right by the gorge entrance, so it is the obvious reward after that walk.

070 411 912 Podhom 62

Gostilna Fortuna

  • Zgornje Gorje
  • Slovenian
  • €€
  • Wine list

The other village tavern, a few minutes further on. Home cooking, a short menu of meat and fish, and a wine list with more thought in it than you expect from a place this size. Quiet on weekday evenings.

Gostilna Murka

  • Bled, 10 km
  • Slovenian
  • €€
  • Garden

A century of doing the same thing well. Trout on the big open grill, grilled meats and vegetables, and a garden set back from the noise of the lake road. This is where to take people who want to eat Slovenian food and mean it.

Riklijeva cesta 9, Bled

Oštarija Peglez'n

  • Bled, 10 km
  • Fish
  • €€
  • 11:00–23:00

Named after an old flat iron, and furnished like a junk shop in the best possible way. Fish is what they do — sea bass, calamari, trout — and the kitchen runs all day, which matters when you come off a mountain at four in the afternoon. Book in summer.

Cesta svobode 19, Bled

Restavracija Sova

  • Bled, 10 km
  • Modern
  • €€€
  • Booking advised

The one for a proper dinner. Seasonal cooking built on local produce, plated with some ambition, and a short menu that changes through the year. Quieter and more grown-up than the lakeside places.

Ljubljanska cesta 8, Bled

Vila Prešeren

  • Bled, 10 km
  • Terrace
  • €€€
  • Cake

A terrace at the water's edge, close enough that the swans come to inspect you. Come for coffee and a slice of kremšnita — the Bled cream cake, invented a few hundred metres from this table — and stay for dinner if the evening is warm.

Veslaška promenada 14, Bled

Gostilna Lectar, Radovljica

  • 15 km
  • Traditional
  • €€
  • Worth the drive

In a house more than five hundred years old on the main square of medieval Radovljica. Big, generous Slovenian cooking upstairs; in the cellar, a workshop still making lect — the decorated honey gingerbread hearts — as it has since 1766. Make an afternoon of it: old town, then lunch.

Linhartov trg 2, Radovljica

Eating out here

  • ◆Book ahead in July and August, especially anywhere on the lake. A phone call in the morning is usually enough.
  • ◆Kitchens close early outside the tourist strip — aim to sit down by half past eight.
  • ◆Order the trout at least once. It comes from these rivers and it is the thing this valley does best.
  • ◆Tipping is not obligatory; rounding up, or ten per cent for good service, is normal.
