About this draft

for the owner

This is a working draft, made to show you before anything is built properly. It sits on a quiet web address that only you have been given, and it is hidden from search engines.

What's real: the address, the check-in times, the facilities and the 1928 story all come from your Booking.com listing. The walks, restaurants and days out are real places, researched and written up.

The photographs of the gorge, the mountains, Bled, Radovljica and the cream cake are real, and freely licensed — every one is credited on the photo credits page.

The pictures of the house are drawn artwork, not photographs. Booking.com blocks automatic downloads, so your own photos are the missing piece — and they will look far better than anything scraped off a listing page.

What I invented: some of the practical wording — the drive, the wood stove, the shifts in the kitchen — to show you how it reads. Change any of it.

What I couldn't know: everything in a dotted yellow box.
