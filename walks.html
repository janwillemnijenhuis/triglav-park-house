Six walks, shortest first

You're in the Radovna valley between two plateaus — Pokljuka above you, Mežakla across the river. From an hour's stroll to a two-thousand-metre summit, all of it starts within half an hour of the house.

Vintgar Gorge & the Šum waterfall

  • 1.6 km
  • 30 min each way
  • Easy
  • Ticket

A boardwalk bolted to the rock, right above the river, ending at the biggest river waterfall in Slovenia. The closest walk to the house and the one everyone remembers.

Up the Radovna valley

  • As far as you like
  • Flat
  • Easy
  • Good on a bike

Follow the river the other way: hay meadows, old farms, and hardly anyone. The walk visitors never find and locals do every Sunday.

Pokljuka Gorge

  • ~2 hours
  • Circular
  • Moderate
  • From Krnica

A dry gorge with wooden galleries, a natural bridge, a cave and a hanging footbridge. Vintgar's wilder, emptier cousin, ten minutes up the road.

Ojstrica & Mala Osojnica

  • 1 hour
  • Steep
  • Moderate
  • The postcard view

Twenty minutes of climbing above Lake Bled buys you the photograph everyone comes to Slovenia for — island, church, mountains behind.

Viševnik, 2050 m

  • 6.4 km return
  • 3 hours
  • 678 m up
  • Strenuous

The easiest two-thousander in the Julian Alps, straight up from Rudno polje, with Triglav itself filling the horizon at the top.

Debela peč, 2014 m

  • 4–5 hours
  • Circular
  • Strenuous
  • Mountain hut

Larch forest, a high pasture with a hut that serves lunch, then a ridge of grassy summits looking straight into the heart of the Alps.

Before you set off

Boots, not trainers, for anything above the valley — the limestone is sharp and it stays slippery long after rain.

Check the forecast in the morning. Alpine thunderstorms build in the afternoon with very little warning. Locals start early and are down by two.

Take water and something to eat. There are no shops on any of these routes, and only Debela peč has a hut.

In an emergency call 112 and ask for mountain rescue. Note where you are before you call.
