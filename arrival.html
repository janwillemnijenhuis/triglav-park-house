The last kilometre

By car. Leave the motorway at Lesce–Bled, follow signs for Bled and then for Gorje. In Zgornje Gorje keep going towards Krnica and the Radovna valley; the house is signed on the right, a few hundred metres up a gravel drive through the trees.

The drive is fine for a normal car in summer. In winter, come slowly — it is a forest track, and it holds snow longer than the main road.

What should guests actually look for at the turn-off? A sign, a barn, a postbox — something they can't miss in the dark.

Good to know on day one

The water. It comes from the mountain and it is better than anything you'll buy in a bottle. Fill your flasks before you walk.

Shopping. There's a small shop in Zgornje Gorje for bread, milk and the essentials; the big supermarkets are in Bled, ten minutes away. Sunday opening is limited across Slovenia, so buy on Saturday.

Which shop in the village, and what are its hours? This is the question guests will ask you most.

Nights are dark and quiet. No street lights. Bring a torch for the drive, or use your phone — and look up, because on a clear night the sky here is extraordinary.
