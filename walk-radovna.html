Up the Radovna valley

the one the locals do

FlatTerrain
1–4 hYour call
YesBikes & prams

Everyone else drives down to the gorge. Turn the other way instead and follow the Radovna upstream, into a long green valley wedged between the Pokljuka and Mežakla plateaus, with the Alps closing in ahead of you.

There is almost no climb. What there is instead: hay meadows cut by hand, wooden barns, a river you can put your feet in, and the sort of quiet that makes people lower their voices.

The route

Start at the gate. Walk up the valley road — it is quiet, paved and nearly level the whole way.

Worth stopping for: the Pocar farmstead in Zgornja Radovna, one of the oldest farms in the valley and now a little museum of how families lived up here. Its rooms are exactly as they were left.

Turn back whenever you like. There is no summit and no ticket — the valley simply gets emptier and prettier the further you go.

Good to know

  • ◆The best evening walk here. Low sun down the length of the valley, and the meadows full of insects.
  • ◆Perfect on a bike. Flat enough for children, and the rare Alpine ride with no climb in it.
  • ◆Cars share the road, but there are very few. Walk on the left and you will hear them coming long before you see them.
  • ◆Dawn and dusk are for deer. Stand still and stay quiet.
