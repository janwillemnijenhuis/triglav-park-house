2050 m — your first two-thousander

3 hMoving time
678 mAscent

If you have never stood on a two-thousand-metre summit, start with this one. It is the most climbed peak of its height in Slovenia, not because it is easy exactly, but because the path is honest all the way up: no cables, no exposure, no route-finding — just a long steady climb that gets rockier as you go.

What you get at the top is out of all proportion to the effort. Triglav straight ahead, the Rjavina ridge, Mali Draški vrh alongside, and the whole Pokljuka plateau spread out green below you.

The route

Drive up to Rudno polje (1347 m), the pass at the top of the Pokljuka road, about half an hour from the house. Large car park at the biathlon centre.

The first half is forest track and easy walking, climbing beside the old ski slope.

The second half steepens onto scree and open mountainside, with the trees thinning out and the views opening behind you.

The summit is a rounded rocky top with a register box. Same way down — and take the scree slowly, it rolls.

Good to know

  • ◆Leave early. Storms build over the Julian Alps in the afternoon. Aim to be off the summit by one o'clock.
  • ◆Proper boots with ankle support, and two litres of water each. There is nowhere to fill up on the mountain.
  • ◆Snow lingers in the upper gullies well into June, and returns in October. Ask before you go outside high summer.
  • ◆Not for young children, and not the day after a big dinner. It is a genuine mountain.
