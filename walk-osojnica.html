Walk 4 · above Lake Bled

Ojstrica & Mala Osojnica

the view you came for

1 hRound trip

SteepEffort

That photograph of Lake Bled — the island with its church, the lake wrapped in forest, the Karavanke behind — is taken from here. It costs twenty minutes of honest uphill, and it is worth every one of them.

Two viewpoints, fifteen minutes apart. Ojstrica is the lower one, framed by pines. Mala Osojnica, higher up, has the wider view and the wooden platform.

The route

Start at Zaka, the quiet western corner of the lake, about fifteen minutes' drive from the house. There is parking by the rowing centre.

Go up to Ojstrica first — twenty steady minutes on a forest path with roots for steps.

Then on to Mala Osojnica, fifteen minutes more, and back down to Zaka in about twenty-five.

Do it in that direction. The Mala Osojnica side involves steep metal stairs, and climbing them is far pleasanter — and safer — than coming down them.

Good to know

  • ◆Not after rain, if you can help it. Those metal stairs and the wet leaves are a bad combination.
  • ◆Sunrise is the prize. Mist on the lake, the church catching the first light, and nobody else there.
  • ◆Short but proper. An hour of real climbing, not a stroll — shoes with grip.
  • ◆Finish with a swim. Zaka has a quiet shore, and in summer the lake is genuinely warm.
