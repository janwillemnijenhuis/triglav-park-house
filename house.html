The kitchen

One proper kitchen, shared by the house, with an oven, a dishwasher and enough pots and pans to cook for everybody at once. Plates, glasses and cutlery for a full table are in the dressers.

With twenty-two people the trick is simple: cook in shifts, and run the dishwasher before you go to bed.
