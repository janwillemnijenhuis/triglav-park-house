Walk 6 · the long one

Debela peč

2014 m, by way of a mountain hut

4–5 hCircular
2014 mSummit
YesHut for lunch

The best full day in the area, and gentler underfoot than Viševnik despite being just as high. You climb through larch forest onto Planina Lipanca, a high pasture with a hut on it, and then walk a grassy ridge from summit to summit with the whole central chain of the Julian Alps in front of you.

The Bled hut on Lipanca serves soup, strudel and beer. Plan your day around it.

The route

Start at the Medvedova konta car park on the Pokljuka plateau, roughly half an hour's drive from the house.

Up to Planina Lipanca through larch woods — a steady, well-graded climb to the pasture and the hut.

On to the ridge. At the Lipanska vrata saddle turn left for Debela peč; turn right and you can add Lipanski vrh.

Make it a circuit by taking in Brda (2009 m) and Mrežce on the way back — three summits for barely any extra climbing, and the reason to come here rather than anywhere else.

Good to know

  • ◆Grassy, not scrambly. The ridge is broad and the paths are good, which makes this the friendliest high day out nearby.
  • ◆Cash for the hut. Mountain huts in Slovenia often prefer it.
  • ◆Watch the weather as you would on any 2000 m ridge — there is nowhere to shelter above the hut.
  • ◆Best from late June to early October, once the snow has gone from the pasture.
