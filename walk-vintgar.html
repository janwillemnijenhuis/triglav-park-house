Vintgar Gorge

and the Šum waterfall

1.6 kmLength
30 minEach way
13 mWaterfall

The Radovna — the same river that runs below the house — has cut a slot through the rock here, and in 1893 the locals bolted a wooden walkway to the wall so people could see it. It has been a favourite ever since, and it is still the best hour you can spend within walking distance of the front door.

You walk directly above the water: green pools, white rapids, and a gorge so narrow in places that the boardwalk crosses from one wall to the other. At the far end the river drops 13 metres over Šum — the largest river waterfall in Slovenia.

The route

Start at the gorge entrance in Podhom, the next hamlet along. Walking there from the house takes about twenty minutes; driving takes five, and there is a car park at the entrance.

Through the gorge is a single path, mostly boardwalk and always beside the river. Half an hour at a gentle pace, longer if you stop for photographs, which you will.

Coming back, either retrace the boardwalk or take the marked path up over the hill, which loops back through the fields with the Karavanke ridge ahead of you. The loop turns it into a comfortable two hours.

Good to know

  • ◆You need a ticket and in summer a timed slot, bought online. Go for the first slot of the morning and you will have the gorge almost to yourself.
  • ◆Seasonal. The gorge opens in spring and closes for winter, when the boardwalk ices over.
  • ◆Not for prams. Boardwalks, steps and a few narrow stretches. Small children on foot are fine; hold hands.
  • ◆There's a tavern at the end — see Gostilna Vintgar, which has been feeding walkers here since the 1800s.

People have been coming here a long time: the boardwalk opened in 1893, and the waterfall was already on postcards.
